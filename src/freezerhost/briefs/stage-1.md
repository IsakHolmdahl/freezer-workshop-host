would have to visit the store to install it. Have your factory build remote software
installation into your cloud service, then use it to bring all ten freezers to 2.0.0.

The full brief is in your repository: `docs/features/remote-software-installation.md`.
Give that file to your factory's coordinator. You do not edit product code yourselves.
